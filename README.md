## Who I am

I started out editing videos, knowing nothing about code. I just wanted to tell stories in a way people would want to watch, and ended up building a community of 30k+ people. That's where I learned something I still carry with me: nobody cares how many features something has, only whether it truly helps, is good to use, and inspires.

Today I study Systems Analysis and Development at Anhanguera, completed ProProfissão by Instituto PROA/Senac (440h in programming and mobile development), and work on both ends: as a **Product Owner**, leading teams of 6 to 7 people and prioritizing the backlog around real impact; and as a **developer**, writing the Kotlin, JavaScript/React and Node.js that bring those ideas to life.

I'm looking for my first internship or junior role in software development.

## Projects

**[IT Purchasing Automation](https://devmoraria.github.io/IT-Purchasing-Automation)** · PO and Dev
A system that queries suppliers (KaBuM, Pichau), compares prices and updates a dashboard in real time. The UiPath bot cut the process from **32h to 2.5h per month**, saving **R$ 2,655.00**.
`Flask` `MongoDB` `UiPath`

**[Virtual Dream](https://github.com/VirtualDream-Demo-Day)** · PO and Dev
Android app for recording and analyzing dreams, with an AI agent (RAG) that interprets each entry and generates images from it. I led a team of 7 people.
`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose` `Node.js` `Express` `Gemini API`

**[Echo Iris](https://echoiris.duckdns.org)** · Dev
Audio player that turns music into real-time 3D visuals, with an AI agent (RAG) that picks the color from the mood of the track. Running on my own Ubuntu server on Oracle Cloud.
`JavaScript` `Three.js` `Flask` `OCI`

**[EducaBem](https://github.com/EducaBem/EducaBem)** (Capstone project with Serasa Experian, in progress) · PO and Dev
A platform that connects donors of financial education books to libraries and NGOs, with a learning quiz. I lead a team of 6 people and implement the Figma prototype in React.
`React` `Figma` `PostgreSQL`

**[ClimaHome Sustentável](https://climahome.com.br)** · Freelancer
Production landing page for a home services company, with a filterable gallery, WhatsApp contact, and domain and DNS configured by me.
`HTML` `CSS` `JavaScript` `Figma`

**[Livrerso](https://github.com/devmoraria/Livrerso)** · Dev
Android book discovery app with a category roulette, using the Open Library API and Amazon integration.
`Kotlin` `Jetpack Compose`
